Strom Clone Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 September 2026

Strom Clone ("Strom", "the app", "we") lets you run a second copy (a clone) of apps that are already installed on your phone, each with its own data and sign-in. This policy explains what information the app handles and why.

Summary

  • Strom does not sell your personal data and has no account system of its own.
  • The free version of Strom shows ads from Google AdMob. AdMob collects some device information to show and measure ads, as explained below.
  • Strom Premium is sold as a subscription through Google Play. Google handles the payment; Strom never sees your card or payment details.
  • Everything else Strom stores stays on your device and is deleted when you uninstall Strom.

Information the app handles on your device

  • List of installed apps. Strom reads the list of apps on your phone (QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES) so you can choose which app to clone. This list is never sent anywhere.
  • Data of cloned apps. Each clone keeps its own files, settings and sign-in inside Strom's private storage on your device. Strom does not read this data for any purpose other than running the clone.
  • Accounts. If you sign in to a Google or other account inside a clone, that account is stored on your device for that clone only.
  • Crash reports. If a clone crashes, Strom saves a technical log on your device so you can view it under Settings > Crash reports. These logs are not uploaded.
  • Premium status. Whether Premium is active, and until when, is stored on your device.

Permissions

Cloned apps run inside Strom, so Android sees Strom as the app that uses a permission. That is why Strom lists permissions such as camera, microphone, location, contacts, calendar, phone, storage, notifications and Bluetooth. Strom itself does not use them to collect information; they are passed on to the cloned app you are using, and Android asks for your permission first where it applies. You can revoke any permission at any time in your phone's settings.

Some permissions have a specific purpose in Strom itself:

  • All files access / storage: to copy a game's extra data (OBB files and asset packs) into its clone.
  • Install packages: to install microG when you choose to enable Google sign-in inside clones.
  • Accounts: to let clones sign in to their own accounts.
  • Foreground services, notifications, battery optimisation: to keep a clone running while you use it.

Advertising

The free version of Strom shows ads, including optional rewarded ads that you can choose to watch to unlock Premium for a limited time. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show, limit and measure ads and to prevent fraud, AdMob may collect:

  • your device's advertising ID, IP address and general (not precise) location;
  • device and app information, such as model, Android version and language;
  • interactions with ads, such as views and taps, and diagnostic data.

How Google uses this information: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA and UK), Strom asks for your consent before ads are personalised. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your phone's Settings > Google > Ads (or Settings > Privacy > Ads). Premium users do not see ads.

Subscriptions and purchases

Strom Premium is sold through Google Play Billing. Google processes the payment under the Google Payments privacy notice. Strom only receives confirmation of your purchase (such as the product, a purchase token and whether the subscription is active) so it can turn Premium on. You can manage or cancel your subscription at any time in the Google Play Store under Payments & subscriptions.

Third-party apps and services

  • Apps you clone work the same as the original apps and may send data to their own servers under their own privacy policies. Strom does not control or see that data.
  • microG (open-source Google services, installed only if you choose to) talks to Google servers when you sign in to a Google account inside a clone. Google's privacy policy applies: policies.google.com/privacy.
  • Share, Rate and Feedback open other apps on your phone (such as Google Play or your email app). Anything you send there is handled by that app.

Children

Strom is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them. Ads in Strom are not intended for children.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling a clone deletes that clone's data. Uninstalling Strom deletes all data Strom stored on your device. Data collected by AdMob is handled by Google; you can reset your advertising ID or request deletion through your Google account settings.

Changes to this policy

If Strom starts handling information differently, this page will be updated and the date above changed.
