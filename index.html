Strom Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 September 2026

Strom ("the app", "we") lets you run a second copy (a clone) of apps that are already installed on your phone, each with its own data and sign-in. This policy explains what information the app handles and why.

Summary

  • Strom does not collect, sell or share your personal data.
  • Strom has no account system, no analytics and no advertising SDKs.
  • Everything Strom stores stays on your device and is deleted when you uninstall Strom.

Information the app handles on your device

  • List of installed apps. Strom reads the list of apps on your phone (QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES) so you can choose which app to clone. This list is never sent anywhere.
  • Data of cloned apps. Each clone keeps its own files, settings and sign-in inside Strom's private storage on your device. Strom does not read this data for any purpose other than running the clone.
  • Accounts. If you sign in to a Google or other account inside a clone, that account is stored on your device for that clone only.
  • Crash reports. If a clone crashes, Strom saves a technical log on your device so you can view it under Settings > Crash reports. These logs are not uploaded.
  • Premium status. Stored on your device only.

Permissions

Cloned apps run inside Strom, so Android sees Strom as the app that uses a permission. That is why Strom lists permissions such as camera, microphone, location, contacts, calendar, phone, storage, notifications and Bluetooth. Strom itself does not use them to collect information; they are passed on to the cloned app you are using, and Android asks for your permission first where it applies. You can revoke any permission at any time in your phone's settings.

Some permissions have a specific purpose in Strom itself:

  • All files access / storage: to copy a game's extra data (OBB files and asset packs) into its clone.
  • Install packages: to install microG when you choose to enable Google sign-in inside clones.
  • Accounts: to let clones sign in to their own accounts.
  • Foreground services, notifications, battery optimisation: to keep a clone running while you use it.

Third-party apps and services

  • Apps you clone work the same as the original apps and may send data to their own servers under their own privacy policies. Strom does not control or see that data.
  • microG (open-source Google services, installed only if you choose to) talks to Google servers when you sign in to a Google account inside a clone. Google's privacy policy applies: policies.google.com/privacy.
  • Share, Rate and Feedback open other apps on your phone (such as Google Play or your email app). Anything you send there is handled by that app.

Children

Strom is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling a clone deletes that clone's data. Uninstalling Strom deletes all data Strom stored on your device.

Changes to this policy

If Strom starts handling information differently (for example if ads or subscriptions are added), this page will be updated and the date above changed.
